The Fringe / Surface survey

Terrain-following freight tracks, mineral beds, weathered formations, and hardware left behind by the Drifters.

Game geometry projected in Canvas; lighting is approximated and vegetation sampled. Junction mode uses a crossing fixture to inspect the shared road layer.

Freight tracks

Worn twin wheel tracks follow the hills. Loose shoulders blend into each biome. Patches of recovered plate mark repairs and colony approaches.

Along the road

Solar relays and reflective route posts. River crossings have wider decks and worn hazard paint.
